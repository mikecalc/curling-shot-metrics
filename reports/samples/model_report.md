## Performance across the corpus

Two kinds of table, by the rule the reports follow. A team's standing is in win probability: its record and where its chance of winning came from, with no execution columns. A player is judged on what they were asked to throw: execution (PGAA, points gained above average) relative to the field for the same shot type and hammer state, read as a distribution rather than an average. `reliability` is the share of shots at or above the field's expectation; `avg_make` how good the shot was when above, `avg_miss` how bad when below; `net` is the mean over every shot, which folds the three together, and the sort; `big_makes_100` and `big_misses_100` are shots beyond half a point either way, per 100 shots. `net_wp` is the same execution in percentage points of win probability per shot, for reference; `call` is the call component (secondary). The full tables are in the leaderboard CSVs.
